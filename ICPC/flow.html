Project Selection

Statement: Suppose there are \( N \) nodes. Picking the \( i \)-th node gives you a score of \( A_i \), while there are prerequisites \( B_{i, j} \) for each node, which if you pick node \( i \), you must pick \( B_{i, j} \) as well. Maximise the score.

We can always rewrite into the following form: Let \( X_i \) be boolean variables, where \( \overline{X_i} \) is the complement (where \( \overline{X_i} = 1 - X_i \)). Maximise the expression \[ \sum_{i} a_i X_i + \sum_{i} b_i \overline{X_i} + \sum_{i \ne j} c_{i, j} X_i \overline{X_j} \] Where \( a_i, b_i, c_{i, j} \ge 0 \) for all \( i, j \). By letting \( s = N \), \( t = N + 1 \) and \( X_s = 1 \) and \( X_t = 0 \), we can further reduce to \[ \sum_{i \ne j} c_{i, j} X_i \overline{X_j} \] By observing \( a_i X_i = a_i X_i \overline{X_t} \) and \( b_i \overline{X_i} = b_i X_s \overline{X_i} \). This is just the min-cut from \( s \) to \( t \) on the graph with edge weights \( c_{i, j} \), noting a \( S \sqcup T \) cut corresponds to the assignment \( X_i = [i \in S] \).
