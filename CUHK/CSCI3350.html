CSCI3350 Introduction to Quantum Computing

Introduction

Why quantum computing? When technology advances, more things we can compute, and therefore we study quantum.

Hilbert Space

Hilbert Space is Complete Complex Inner-Product Space. A qubit (quantum-bit) is defined to be a vector in Hilbert Space of deminsion 2. The standard basis (e_n) is often used.

The Four Postulates

  1. A quantum physical system is described by a state vector, which is an unit vector in some Hilbert Space.
